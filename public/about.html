Our Mission

FreeHosts was created to help hobbyists, students, and makers quickly find free hosting options for small projects and learning. We believe everyone should have access to the tools they need to bring their ideas to life without financial barriers.

Our focus is on providing clear, accurate listings and community-contributed insights so people can make informed decisions and get their projects online fast. We're committed to maintaining a trustworthy, up-to-date directory that serves the community.

Our Values

Community First

Built by the community, for the community. Every contribution matters and helps others succeed.

Transparency

Honest reviews, clear information, and open communication about our processes and decisions.

Quality Over Quantity

We carefully curate listings to ensure every host meets our standards for reliability and usefulness.

Free & Accessible

Our directory will always be free. No paywalls, no premium tiers, just helpful resources for everyone.

How You Can Help

FreeHosts thrives because of community contributions. Here's how you can make a difference:

  • Suggest Hosts: Join our Discord and post in the "add-host" channel with provider details, features, and links.
  • Share Reviews: Help others by sharing your experiences with different hosting providers.
  • Report Issues: Let us know if you find outdated information or broken links so we can keep listings accurate.
  • Share Tips: Post your setup guides, tips, and best practices to help newcomers get started.
  • Spread the Word: Tell others about FreeHosts to help grow our community.

Our Story

FreeHosts started as a simple idea: make it easier for people to find reliable free hosting without endless searching and comparing.

The Beginning

Started as a small list shared among friends to help each other find free hosting for hobby projects.

Growing Community

Word spread, and more people joined to share their experiences and suggestions, forming the foundation of our community.

Launch of Directory

Built a proper directory website with reviews, ratings, and detailed information to serve the growing community better.

Today

A thriving community of 400+ members with 100+ curated hosting providers, helping thousands find the right hosting solution.
